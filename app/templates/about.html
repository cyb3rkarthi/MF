Experience that turns culinary concepts into enduring, profitable food businesses.

Madras Foodies Consultancy is a Chennai-based café, restaurant, and hotel advisory firm founded by Yuvaraj, a seasoned hospitality leader with 27+ years of hands-on industry expertise across India and international markets.

Leadership & Background

27 years of deep industry mastery across dining, cafés, bars, and catering.

Founder Yuvaraj holds professional credentials including a Diploma in Hotel Management & Catering Technology, a Bachelor's degree in Hotel Management and Catering Technology, and specialized certification in Bar Tending & Beverage Operations.

His career spans nearly three decades of active consulting and culinary project leadership across India, alongside 3 years of executive consulting in Malaysia. He specializes in concept ideation, ergonomic commercial kitchen design, recipe standardization, and high-efficiency food service management.
